### Special Routes (Non-Section, Acceptable)

| Route      | Status  | Notes                                                |
| ---------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `/`        | Present | App root/landing                                     |
| `/player/` | Present | Player mode — standalone persona, not section-rooted |

### Findings

1. **10 legacy routes exist as full duplicate implementations** instead of redirects. This
   doubles maintenance burden and risks behavior drift between legacy and canonical versions.
2. All canonical section routes are present and functional.
3. Section landing pages (`/atlas/`, `/campaign/`, `/session/`) correctly redirect to their
   primary content route.

### Remediation Items (S21.4.2)

- **REM-2.1** (P2): Convert all 10 legacy routes to `goto()` redirects targeting canonical
  section routes, preserving query parameters. Remove duplicate implementations.
- **REM-2.2** (P3): Add a route audit test that enumerates `src/routes/` and asserts no
  non-section top-level routes exist (except `/`, `/player/`).
