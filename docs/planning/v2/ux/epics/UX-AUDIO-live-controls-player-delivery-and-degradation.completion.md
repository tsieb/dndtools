## Summary

Re-themed and token-polished the audio playback surface (`AudioPlayback`) — fixed its hardcoded
light-only fallback colours and gave the live controls, now-playing readout, delivery roster/queue,
and Scene-preset list proper token styling. Every functional `audio-*` testid is preserved.

- **UX-AUDIO-001/002 live controls + now playing:** the playback form, transport controls, and the
  now-playing readout become token controls (44px) with an accent-bordered now-playing card.
- **UX-AUDIO-003 player delivery:** the delivery roster + queue (incl. offline-queued projection)
  become raised token rows.
- **UX-AUDIO-008 degradation:** the reduced-motion crossfade behaviour is retained (no animation when
  motion is reduced); error/missing/blocked preset states read in the error tone.
- **UX-AUDIO-009/012 presets/association:** the Scene-preset list + associate form are token-styled;
  the DM-only preset section stays hidden from players.
