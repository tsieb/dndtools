# Settings

Make Lamplight fit your table: how it looks, how much it shows, and where your campaign is kept.

## Find a setting

Open **Settings** and choose a section. **Appearance** holds the theme, interface density and **Experience complexity**, which decides how many tools are on screen. Start with Beginner or Standard and move up when you want more; nothing you have made is lost when tools are hidden. **Language & region** switches the app's language straight away.

<!-- keyboard -->

With a keyboard, Tab moves through the section list and the setting rows. **Accessibility** lists every keyboard shortcut, and ? shows the same list anywhere. To get here from anywhere, press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) and type Settings.

<!-- touch -->

On a phone, **Settings** is under **More** in the tab bar. Choose a section from the **Settings section** menu at the top of the page.

## Your campaign and your players

**Backup & history** exports a copy of your vault and holds the privacy mode, cloud backup and recovery-key controls; the **Privacy modes** guide explains them. **Vault connections** links a folder of notes. **Players** names the people at your table and **Permissions** decides what each of them can see and change.

## Comfort and safety

**Accessibility** turns on reduced motion and the high-contrast theme, and runs player-safety checks that confirm GM-only scenes, notes and handouts are hidden from every player view. **About & diagnostics** shows the version you are running and, in the desktop app, checks for updates.
