# Notes

Write it down once and find it when the players ask. Notes hold your preparation, your handouts and the text you read aloud.

## Write and link

Open **Notes** and choose **New note**. Type two square brackets, [[, to link another note by its title; the link shows up under **Backlinks** on the other note, so related material finds itself. Typing a slash opens the insert menu for tables, callouts, dice rolls, templates and snippets; a secret callout is removed from every player copy. Notes save as you type.

**Templates** starts a note from a ready-made outline, and **Snippets** keeps the passages you paste often. **Story** holds quests, factions and the campaign calendar, and **Graph & search** shows how everything connects once you have a few linked notes. It also offers to repair a link whose note was renamed or removed.

## Find it again

**Search** filters notes by words, kind and tag. Save a search you run often and it appears in the command palette too.

<!-- keyboard -->

With a keyboard, press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) anywhere and type a note's title to open it, or a saved search's name to run it.

<!-- touch -->

On a phone, **Notes** is under **More** in the tab bar. Tap **Search** in the top bar and type a note's title to open it from anywhere, or a saved search's name to run it.

## Share with the table

A new note is yours alone. **Push to players**, or the **Note visibility** control, shows it to players after a confirmation: once they have read it, hiding it again does not take it back. To bring in a folder of existing notes, use **Sources** and **Import vault**.
